## Style

Classical, restrained, institutional, historical, slightly traditional.

Avoid:
- Generic futuristic SaaS look
- Excessive gradients
- Neon
- Excessive animation
- Pseudo-occult theatrical imagery invented by AI
- Arbitrary gold palette
- Decorative Masonic symbols not supplied/approved by the client

Use real Astrea branding rather than generic "Masonic" visual cliches.

## Primary Visual Asset

Astrea standard/banner supplied by client.

## Hero Intent

Dark surface, standard positioned left or right, subtle light behind it, restrained typography.
